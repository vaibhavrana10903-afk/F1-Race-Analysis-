What the workbook analyses

Each view is a worksheet in the Tableau file, driven by columns from the race results table.

Driver reliability

Retirements per driver, broken down by reason, plus DNF trends season by season.

dnf · dnf_reason · driver

Team reliability

DNF rate by team and by circuit type, to show where cars fail most.

SUM(dnf) / COUNT(race_id)

Podium achievements

Podiums by driver and team, counting any finish in the top three.

finish_position <= 3

Tyre strategy

Average points by starting compound, and how tyre strategies split across races.

starting_compound · tyre_strategy

Seasonal team points

How each constructor’s points total moved from season to season.

season · team · points

Race participation

Where Grand Prix entries were logged, by country on a world map.

COUNTD(country)

About the data

Source: a Tableau workbook built on the “F1 Racing Project Dataset” (sheet race_results, 6,464 rows, 42 columns: season, round, circuit, driver, team, grid and finish position, points, DNF reason, pit stops, tyre strategy, weather and more). The uploaded workbook holds the structure only, so figures here are the values shown on its dashboard. Team bars other than Red Bull Racing are scaled from the dashboard chart.
